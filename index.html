Services

One contract, from survey to handover

We take HVAC and MEP projects end to end, so you deal with one accountable team throughout.

  • Survey & feasibility

    Condition, damper and ventilation surveys that give you a clear picture before you commit.

  • MEP design & coordination

    Mechanical, electrical and plumbing design, coordinated across trades and with your project team.

  • Project management

    Project, site, commercial and health & safety management, led by senior people who stay hands-on.

  • Delivery

    Mechanical, electrical, HVAC, plumbing and drainage installation, with building fabric works through our trusted specialist network.

  • Commissioning & validation

    Ventilation, water systems and controls integration, commissioned and validated to specification.

  • Handover & documentation

    Complete, organised handover information so your systems are ready to operate and maintain.

Also

Specialist plant replacement, temporary plant, gas purges, lifecycle and CapEx support, and pre-medical imaging room preparation for MRI and CT.
